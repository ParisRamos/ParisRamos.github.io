## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an executive command-center visual identity tailored for high-stakes leadership across applied data science, financial engineering, and strategic governance. Rooted in deep navy foundational tones with surgical strikes of high-contrast emerald and cobalt blue, the aesthetic radiates rigorous analytical precision, sovereign stability, and transformative institutional impact.

The visual dialect bridges high-level administrative authority with quantitative computational power. Rather than relying on generic corporate flatness, the interface operates like an advanced financial intelligence instrument: dense with structural logic, crisp in visual hierarchy, and uncompromising in legibility. The mood is measured, visionary, and decisive—instilling immediate confidence among C-suite executives, institutional boards, and governmental leaders.

## Colors

The palette balances deep naval structure with high-potency kinetic markers:

- **Primary (`#059669` - Emerald Signal):** The signature accent of economic alpha and validated computational impact. Deployed strictly for primary strategic conversions, critical execution metrics, active states, and positive performance deltas.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB` - Cobalt Steel):** Represents structural technological infrastructure and operational scale. Used for secondary system actions, data ingestion channels, and relational links.
- **Tertiary (`#0EA5E9` - Cyber Cerulean):** Reserved for contextual insights, analytical indicators, dynamic charts, and micro-telemetry data tags.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0F172A` - Deep Obsidian Slate):** The primary canvas ground, supplemented with calibrated sub-layers:
  - Surface Foundation: `#0F172A`
  - Elevated Module: `#1E293B`
  - Accent / Raised Tier: `#334155`
  - Border Gridline: `rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.16)`
- **Typography & Data Tones:**
  - High-Contrast Metric/Display: `#F8FAFC`
  - Structural Body / Secondary: `#94A3B8`
  - De-emphasized Telemetry: `#64748B`

Color is never decorative; it functions as analytical state architecture where hue instantly communicates system validity, capital yield, and infrastructure reliability.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples the angular, structural geometry of **Space Grotesk** with the razor-sharp mechanical legibility of **Geist**.

- **Space Grotesk (Display & Headlines):** Carries an unmistakable computational authority. It evokes institutional terminals and advanced mathematical modeling without surrendering executive polish. Display headings use tight tracking and controlled line heights to convey density and deliberate focus.
- **Geist (Body & Technical Labels):** Engineered for exceptional optical legibility at dense data intervals. Numbers, financial deltas, and multi-clause institutional case summaries render with absolute neutral clarity.
- **Tabular Numerics:** For all economic figures, algorithmic performance rates, and project budgets, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` must be enforced to maintain rigid vertical data alignment across summaries and dashboards.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to a strict 12-column grid anchored by an 8pt structural rhythm, with dynamic reflow rules ensuring institutional legibility across varied viewport constraints:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid architecture constrained to a max-width of 1360px. Outer margins sit at `margin-lg` (5rem) with `gutter` (1.5rem). Modules, metrics, and evidence briefs distribute across asymmetrical spans (e.g., 8-column analytical case studies paired with 4-column KPI telemetry sidebars).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with `margin-md` (3rem) and `gutter-sm` (1rem). High-level statistics collapse into 2x2 grids, and dual-column portfolio projects shift to stacked cards.
- **Mobile (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with `margin` (1.5rem) and `gutter-sm` (1rem). Dense computational metrics stack vertically with distinct divider lines to preserve tap target margins and rapid vertical scanning.

Component density is compact yet deliberate: padding values (`space-sm` through `space-xl`) frame content tightly without visual clutter, emphasizing high information density over decorative emptiness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use blur-heavy lighting or soft consumer drop shadows. Depth is achieved via architectural luminance stacking and crisp, calibrated boundary thresholds:

- **Base Ground (`#0F172A`):** The foundational substrate representing system level zero.
- **Elevated Analytical Layer (`#1E293B`):** Applied to cards, executive dossiers, and data containers. Delineated by a structural outer border: `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`.
- **Interactive Focus / Active Tier (`#334155`):** Reserved for hovering cards, pinned projects, and active modal shells, bolstered by an outer glow of `0 0 0 1px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.45)` and a directional shadow of `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`.
- **Luminance Inlays:** Micro-panels and code/metric monitors employ an inset tonal backdrop (`#090D16`) with an inner boundary `inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)` to create the illusion of an embedded instrumentation readout.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a disciplined, quasi-industrial corner geometry (`roundedness: 1`):

- **Micro Controls & Chips:** `0.25rem` (4px). Provides clean, geometric precision reminiscent of command terminal switches.
- **Cards, System Surfaces & Modals:** `0.5rem` (8px). Ensures self-contained architectural separation without softening the visual impact.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted entirely to live telemetry state badges (e.g., "DEPLOYED SYSTEM", "PRODUCTION LIVE") to mimic military and financial hardware indicator LEDs.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Executive Action / Contact):** Background `#059669`, foreground `#F8FAFC`, font `Geist` medium (0.875rem), radius `0.25rem`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Subtle hover transition to `#047857` coupled with a localized emerald boundary pulse (`0 0 12px rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary (Technical Case Dossier / Methodology):** Background `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.4)`, foreground `#93C5FD`. Hover changes background to `rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.2)` and border color to `#2563EB`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background with `#94A3B8` foreground and a bottom accent border appearing solely on focus or hover.

### Metric Cards & Impact Panels
- **Container:** Background `#1E293B`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.12)`, padding `space-lg`.
- **Header:** Features a top horizontal track showing a micro-kicker in uppercase `label-sm` tracking with a status pip (`#059669`).
- **Data Display:** Large Space Grotesk numeral in `#F8FAFC` (`display-mobile` to `display`), paired with an upward trend delta marker in `#059669`.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- **Base State:** Background `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.8)`, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`, text `#94A3B8`, uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Selected State:** Background `rgba(5, 150, 105, 0.15)`, border `1px solid #059669`, text `#34D399`.

### Form Fields & Strategic Inquiries
- **Input Ground:** `#090D16` inset background, border `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`, text `#F8FAFC`, font `Geist` (1rem).
- **Focus:** Sharp border transition to `#2563EB` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 2px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.25)`. Error states replace borders with `#EF4444`.

### Interactive Project & Solution Rows
- Structured analytical lists containing: Solution Scope (Column 1), Implementation Architecture (Column 2), Quantifiable Yield/Alpha (Column 3), and Actionable Audit Link (Column 4).
- Divided by horizontal lines of `1px solid rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.08)`. Rows elevate dynamically to `#1E293B` on hover with a lateral `#059669` 2px border indicator on the leading edge.
